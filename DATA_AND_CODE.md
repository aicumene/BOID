## AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset

The **AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset** is a research object separate from the
BOID software and is archived on Zenodo:

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22923070
- Companion repository:
  https://github.com/aicumene/aicumene-eeg-bci-sleep-wake-dataset

The dataset is organized into two components:

1. a **public synthetic-data component** for reproducible validation and testing;
2. **recordings acquired with AiCumene neurointerface devices**, available to
   qualified researchers upon request, subject to the applicable access, privacy,
   and data-use conditions.

The dataset does not define BOID. It is used to test and validate the neuroscience
application of BOID, including state-dependent changes in oscillator dimensionality
across sleep/wake states.
