# Figure sources

All figures in this directory are extracted from manuscripts supplied by the authors.
Only page margins were cropped; the scientific plots themselves were not redrawn.

## General BOID / dimensionality foundation

Source:

Alexandra Bernadotte. **Topology-driven classification of time series.**
bioRxiv (2026). DOI: 10.64898/2026.04.25.720787.

**Author / rights holder for these extracted figures:** Alexandra Bernadotte.  
**Figure copyright:** © Alexandra Bernadotte.

- `bernadotte_fig2_embedding_dimension.png` — Figure 2, PDF page 15.
  Delay-embedding examples illustrating growth of embedding dimension with additional exponential components.
- `bernadotte_fig7_oscillator_planes.png` — Figure 7, PDF page 19.
  Harmonic superposition and characteristic low-dimensional projections associated with oscillatory components.
- `bernadotte_fig10_modulated_oscillations.png` — Figure 10, PDF page 22.
  Exponentially modulated oscillations and their spiral-like delay-embedding geometry.
- `bernadotte_fig16_regime_geometry.png` — Figure 16, PDF page 29.
  Geometric separation of signals generated by different dynamical regimes.

The bioRxiv version states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The repository use is by the author/rightsholder.

## Neuroscience application

Source:

Alexandra Bernadotte, Ivan Menshikov. **Brain states are organized by regulated oscillator dimensionality.**
Manuscript, 2026.

**Authors / rights holders for these manuscript figures:** Alexandra Bernadotte and Ivan Menshikov.  
**Figure copyright:** © Alexandra Bernadotte and Ivan Menshikov.

- `boid_fig1_neuroscience_framework.png` — Figure 1, PDF page 5.
  Brain-specific BOID application framework: organized low-dimensional states versus pathological loss of dynamical control.
- `boid_figS1_sleep_trajectories.png` — Figure S1, PDF page 43.
  Whole-night BOID trajectories across the Sleep-EDF human sleep sample.

Note: Figure 1 comes from the neuroscience manuscript and therefore uses the application-specific
phrase “Brain Oscillator Intrinsic Dimension.” The general method name used in this repository is
**Bernadotte Oscillator Intrinsic Dimension (BOID)**.
